# Student Performance Factors — DEPI Final Project

A group data analysis project exploring the factors that influence student exam performance — including study habits, attendance, parental involvement, family income, and gender.

---

## What We Explored

We analyzed a real-world dataset of student performance factors and built visualizations to answer key questions:

- Does studying more hours actually improve exam scores?
- How does attendance affect performance?
- Do parental involvement and motivation level play a role?
- Is there a difference in performance between genders?
- How does family income relate to student outcomes?
- Does school type (public vs. private) affect how much students study?

---

## Visualizations

| # | Chart | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | School Type by Education Level & Gender | Bar chart comparing study hours across school types, split by parental education and gender |
| 2 | Exam Scores by Hours Studied | Area + line chart showing the relationship between study time and average scores, by gender |
| 3 | Scores Over Study Hours | Simple trend line of average scores vs. hours studied |
| 4 | Scores by Attendance | How attendance rate correlates with exam performance |
| 5 | Parental & Environmental Influence | Stacked bar of parental involvement vs. exam scores, colored by motivation level |
| 6 | Family Income Distribution | Pie chart of exam score distribution across income brackets and gender |
| 7 | Scores by Gender | Pie chart comparing total exam score share between male and female students |
